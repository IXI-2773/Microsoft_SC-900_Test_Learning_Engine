### Attack

This was a real blocker for CAND-01R2 because R2 retained the historical profile as a SEED prior for initial emphasis.

Direct SC-900 evidence remains required before any later historical-prior re-enable.

## N08 — LOAD_BEARING_ASSUMPTION SELF-ATTACK

### Attack

The project must not smuggle assumptions such as personalization⇒learning, more signals⇒better policy, local correctness⇒exam competence, or governance⇒scientific validity into implementation authority.

### Reopening finding

The current candidate no longer requires any of those assumptions to be true:

- Smart Practice remains champion, not presumed winner;
- RRC-1 is a challenger, not presumed winner;
- primary success is clean delayed held-out SC-900 performance, not repeated-bank accuracy;
- the candidate bank remains non-default until later authorization;
- TRAIN/PROBE isolation is explicit;
- RRC-1 fairness/starvation is instrumented by design rather than assumed;
- causal/confound limitations are predeclared;
- the cross-exam historical prior has been removed from initial runtime authority by CAND-01R3;
- no population/general efficacy claim is authorized from one learner.

A controlled comparison is still required before claiming learning effectiveness. That is the purpose of later implementation/measurement, not evidence that the design attack was ignored.
