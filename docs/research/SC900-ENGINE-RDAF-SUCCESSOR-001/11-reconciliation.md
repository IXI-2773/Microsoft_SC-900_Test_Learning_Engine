# Phase 12 — Reconciliation (after Route A and Route B each stand alone)

Do not average genuine disagreements.

## AGREEMENTS

1. Retrieval practice with feedback is the engine's legitimate core.
2. Spacing for delayed retention is supported; current numbers are uncalibrated.
3. Exam-mode withheld feedback is reasonable as simulation.
4. Due review and weak retest are simple and aligned with evidence families.
5. Operational IRT/CAT/DKT are **not** justified on this product shape.
6. Unseen / held-out success is the right criterion; same-item accuracy is not exam readiness.
7. One-learner item difficulty/discrimination/quality are not identified.
8. No runtime implementation is authorized by this epoch's data.
9. Do not turn the app into RDAF.

## BOUNDARY_CONDITIONS

- Evidence applies to **delayed** tests more than to session HUD accuracy.
- MC certification ≠ free-recall labs ≠ algebra ITS.
- Bank size 8 makes all empirical engine claims **out of sample**.
- Single-user desktop ≠ CAT program.

## CURRENT_ENGINE_ASSUMPTIONS_REJECTED

- Difficulty rows = item difficulty
- Knowledge-trace rows = KT
- Pass predictor = exam pass probability
- Graph diagnoses operate in ordinary use
- Policy promotion is an empirical optimizer here
- More analytics dimensions = more learning

## CURRENT_ENGINE_ASSUMPTIONS_SURVIVED

- Practice answering beats rereading (as a product choice; not A/B'd here)
- Some spacing/due mechanism should exist
- Feedback after study answers should exist
- Exam simulation should exist
- Letter-position shuffle is useful
- Quarantine/suspend of bad items is useful **when a human flags them**
- Persistence features (save/resume) are load-bearing **software**, not learning claims
