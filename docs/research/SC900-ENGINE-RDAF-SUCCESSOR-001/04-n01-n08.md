## N01 INTUITION_OR_NONTRADITIONAL_HYPOTHESIS_GENERATION

STATE = ACTIVE  
MATERIALITY = MATERIAL  
BASIS = Reconstruction anomalies plus certification-prep specifics, generated before treating literature as confirmation.  
INPUTS_CONSIDERED = frozen modules listed in `02-current-engine-reconstruction.md`; 8-item bank; single-learner desktop shape; SC-900 MC fundamentals blueprint.  
OUTPUTS_OR_REFS =

Hypotheses generated (not yet claimed true):

1. **Graph theater:** empty runtime edges make "root-cause" language a UI/analytics costume.
2. **Readiness as false precision:** a 0.1-resolution score with labels Likely ready is more dangerous than a coarse dashboard.
3. **Massed repair paradox:** follow-up twins after errors implement the opposite of the spacing the due-role claims to optimize.
4. **Dump-training risk:** if a future ingested bank overlaps exam-prep dumps, Smart Practice that prefers "high utility seen-weak items" will maximize item memorization, not concept transfer.
5. **Measurement already exists but is unused:** 24h/7d Brier/ECE could be the scheduler's objective; instead a different heuristic pass-predictor is shown to users.
6. **Exam Sure-contamination:** exam mode silently writes confidence=Sure, poisoning calibration.
7. **Utility non-identifiability:** 8 bounded components plus 50+ profile knobs cannot be uniquely fit from one learner.

REOPEN_OR_RESOLUTION_CONDITION = A later epoch that tests H1–H7 against delayed unseen-item outcomes, or rejects them with contrary evidence.

## N04 ALIEN_OR_CROSS_DISCIPLINARY_PERSPECTIVES

STATE = ACTIVE  
MATERIALITY = MATERIAL  
BASIS = Engine is written in ITS/analytics language; other fields constrain it.  
INPUTS_CONSIDERED = psychometrics (IRT/CAT/exposure); human factors (RT, fatigue, accessibility); decision science (calibration, overconfidence); recommender systems (explore/exploit, popularity bias); causal inference (no identification of item vs person).  
OUTPUTS_OR_REFS =

- Psychometrics: one-person difficulty is not *b*; CAT is not "pick high utility."
- Human factors: 45s latency ≠ non-mastery; accessibility (screen readers, extra reading time) would be punished.
- Decision science: readiness labels are choice architecture, not just numbers (see P5).
- Recommenders: freshness/repetition_cost resembles popularity/diversity reranking, not knowledge tracing.
- Causal: source-trust and item-quality are not identified from one learner's errors.

REOPEN_OR_RESOLUTION_CONDITION = Successor design that ignores exposure control, accessibility-RT confounding, or label-induced overconfidence.

## N08 LOAD_BEARING_ASSUMPTION_SELF_ATTACK

STATE = ACTIVE  
MATERIALITY = MATERIAL  
BASIS = If these assumptions fail, Smart Practice 9 and readiness analytics lose their justification.  
INPUTS_CONSIDERED = precommitment; Route B; reconstruction.  
OUTPUTS_OR_REFS = Attacks:

1. **Personalization ⇒ learning.** False in general; personalization can overfit to seen items (exposure) and starve coverage.
2. **More signals ⇒ better policy.** Correlated features inflate apparent sophistication; Gervet/Pelánek: simple logistic often wins at small data.
3. **Local correctness ⇒ exam competence.** MC recognition of seen stems is the wrong criterion for a closed-book vendor exam with unseen items.
4. **The utility is a model of learning.** It is a designer ranking function. No loss was minimized against delayed outcomes.
5. **Governance makes it scientific.** An unused promotion gate does not validate the active bootstrap policy.
6. **We can estimate item quality from one user.** We cannot.
7. **Repair implies misconception ontology.** We have error flags, not diagnosed misconceptions.

None of these attacks are fully empirically proven **against this binary**, because the bank cannot host the experiment. They are strong enough to **forbid implementation authorization** until a measurable comparison exists.

REOPEN_OR_RESOLUTION_CONDITION = Controlled comparison on delayed unseen-item outcomes with a real bank. Until then, N08 remains ACTIVE and blocks Gate 3.
